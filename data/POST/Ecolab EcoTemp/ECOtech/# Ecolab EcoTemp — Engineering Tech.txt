# 13. Error Codes

| ---- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| E1   | Water supply error | supply valve / strainer / solenoid |
| E2   | Drain error        | sump / drain hose / pump           |
| E3   | Heating error      | element / thermostat / relay       |

# 14. Common Technical Problems

---

## Machine Not Powering

Check

* circuit breaker
* heater short
* wiring
* door switch

---

## Poor Cleaning

Check

* clogged spray nozzles
* low water level
* cavitation
* dirty sump filter

---

## Continuous Drain

Possible causes

* drain ball failure
* debris in drain seat
* bent drain rod

---

## Continuous Cycling

Check

* DELIME mode
* controller stuck in FILL
* timer motor failure
* solenoid relay
