Zanussi Professional Warewashing Engineering Specifications

Brand Context:
Zanussi Professional brand within Electrolux Professional group.

Machine Architectures:
Undercounter Front-Loading Series (Z-Active 100).
Hood-Type Pass-Through Series (ZHT8, ZHT8I, ZHT8TI).

Applications:
Undercounter: restaurants cafes pubs medium-volume kitchens.
Hood-Type: canteens hospitals large kitchens.

Rack System:
Standard rack size 500×500 mm.

Loading Capacity:
Z-Active 100 loading height 400 mm.
Maximum plate diameter 360 mm.
Maximum glass height 325 mm.

ZHT8 Series loading height 440 mm.
Maximum plate diameter >400 mm.
Maximum glass height 400 mm.

Connectivity:
Z-Active 100 integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth with OnE Connected App.
ZHT8 optional digital interface.

Performance:

Z-Active 100
Maximum productivity 65 racks/hour (~1170 dishes/hour).
Fast cycle 55 s.
Standard cycle 100 s.
Intensive cycle 120 s.
Water consumption 2.1–2.4 L per rack.
Eco productivity approx 30–36 racks/hour.

ZHT8 Series
Maximum productivity 80 racks/hour (~1440 dishes/hour).
Cycle durations 45 s / 84 s / 150 s.
NSF mode productivity 63 racks/hour (~1134 dishes/hour).
Water consumption approx 2.0 L per rack.

Hydraulic System:

Z-Active 100
Wash pump power 0.736 kW.
Wash tank capacity 22 L.
Rinse pressure 2–5 bar (pressure boiler).
Spray arms composite material removable clip-on design.
Filtration removable tank filters.

ZHT8 Series
Wash pump power 0.8 kW.
Wash tank capacity 24 L.
Rinse pressure constant 6 bar using atmospheric boiler + rinse pump.
Spray arms stainless steel interchangeable.
Advanced multi-stage filtration.

Thermal System:

Wash temperature 55–65°C.
NSF wash temperature up to 75°C.

Z-Active 100
Boiler capacity 5.8 L.
Boiler heater 2.8 kW.
Tank heater 2.0 kW.
Final rinse temperature 60–85°C.

ZHT8 Series
Boiler capacity 12 L.
Boiler heater 9.0 kW.
Tank heater 3.0 kW.
Final rinse temperature constant 84°C.
Energy Saving Device (ESD) heat recovery on ZHT8I/ZHT8TI models.

Electrical Specifications:

Z-Active 100 (Model 400302)
Supply voltage 220–240 V.
Phase configuration single phase.
Frequency 50 Hz.
Installed power approx 3.3 kW.
Plug type Schuko plug.
Protection rating IPx4.

ZHT8 Series (Example model 505108)
Supply voltage 400 V / 3N.
Convertible to 230 V single phase or three phase.
Frequency 50 Hz.
Installed power 9.9 kW.
Reducible power mode 6.9 kW.
Protection rating IP25.

Mechanical Components:

Deep drawn AISI 304 stainless steel wash tank.
AISI 304 stainless steel body panels.
Double skin insulated door (undercounter).
Single skin or double skin insulated hood (ZHT8I/ZHT8TI).
Adjustable feet up to +50 mm.

Noise Levels:
Z-Active 100 <70 dBA.
ZHT8I/ZHT8TI approx <63 dBA.

Dimensions:

Z-Active 100 (Model 400302)
Width 572 mm.
Depth 623 mm.
Height 830 mm.
Internal chamber 520×540×400 mm.
Net weight approx 54 kg.
Shipping weight approx 68 kg.

ZHT8 Series
Width 752 mm.
Depth 755 mm.
Height closed 1547 mm.
Height open >2000 mm.
Internal chamber height 440 mm.
Net weight approx 106 kg.
Shipping weight approx 122 kg.

Sensors and Control Systems:

Microprocessor control platform.
NTC temperature probes (tank and boiler).
Pressure switches for water level control.
Safety thermostats for heater protection.
Optional flow meters for water monitoring.

Error Codes:

i10 / i11 – water inlet timeout.
i20 / C2 – drain timeout.
i30 – flood protection activation.
i40 / i43 – pressure sensor fault.
i50 – wash pump motor fault.
i60 – heating failure (tank or boiler).
iF0 – filter clogging or foam detection.
iC0 – UI to main board communication failure.

Chemical Dosing System:

Peristaltic detergent and rinse aid pumps.

Dosing Parameters:
dIn initial detergent 0–240 s default 90 s.
rIn initial rinse aid 0–180 s default 10 s.
dEt detergent per cycle 0–182 s default 8 s.
rAi rinse aid per cycle 0–62 s default 4 s.

Installation Requirements:

Water inlet connection 3/4".
Water pressure 0.5–7 bar (2–5 bar recommended).
Recommended inlet water temperature 55–65°C.
Minimum inlet temperature 10°C.
Drain connection 40 mm gravity drain.
Drain pump outlet approx 20.5 mm.
Maximum drain height approx 750 mm.

Clearance Requirements:

Hood-type machines require vertical clearance ≥2100 mm.

Equipotential bonding connection provided.

Certifications:

NSF/ANSI 3 sanitation compliance.
CE certification.
UL certification.
DIN 10512 commercial dishwashing hygiene standard.
Ingress protection ratings IPx4 (undercounter) and IP25 (hood-type).

Materials:

AISI 304 stainless steel structural components.
Deep drawn seamless tank construction.
Double skin insulation for thermal efficiency and noise reduction.
