Hobart Commercial Warewashing Engineering Knowledge Base; Platforms LX Series (LX / LXi / LXe / LXn / LXGn), AM Series (AM-12 / AM-14 / AM-15 / AM-16), CL Series (CL44e / CL54e / CL64e), FT Series (FT-1000e).

Water Supply Engineering: allowable supply pressure 15–65 PSI; optimal operating pressure approx 20 PSI; pressure regulator required if supply >65 PSI; high-temperature models minimum supply temperature approx 110°F; chemical sanitizing models recommended inlet 120–140°F.

Rinse System Engineering: rinse system types line-pressure rinse or pumped rinse; LX rinse pump centrifugal type motor approx 0.19 HP; rinse pressure approx 15–25 PSI; rinse flow LX series approx 0.62–0.74 gal per rack (≈2.3–2.8 L); AM series approx 0.74–1.2 gal per rack; Advansys AM-16 approx 0.58–0.67 gal per rack; CL conveyor approx 1.8–2.1 GPM; FT-1000e approx 58–120 GPH.

Sanitizing Temperatures: wash tank temperature approx 150°F chemical models approx 160°F high-temperature models; final rinse sanitizing temperature approx 180°F (82°C); booster heater temperature rise capability approx 70°F; Sense-A-Temp logic interlock prevents rinse cycle start until booster temperature reached.

Wash Pump Systems: centrifugal wash pump configuration; LXe / LXn wash pump motor approx 0.85 HP; LX-18 / LX-30 pump approx 0.75 HP flow approx 35 GPM peak pressure approx 10–12 PSI; LXe / LXn pump flow approx 38 GPM peak approx 12 PSI; AM-15 / AM-16 pump approx 2.0 HP flow approx 160 GPM peak approx 15 PSI; CL44e / CL54e pump approx 2.0 HP flow approx 202 GPM peak approx 18 PSI; CL64e prewash pump approx 1.0 HP flow approx 150 GPM peak approx 8–10 PSI; PW-10 / PW-20 pump approx 2–5 HP flow ≥250 GPM peak approx 25 PSI.

Hydraulic Architecture: recirculating wash system wash pump → wash manifold → upper/lower rotating wash arms → tank recirculation; final rinse circuit water supply → booster heater → rinse pump → rinse arms → tank refresh → drain.

Hydraulic Operating Pressure: wash nozzle pressure approx 12–15 PSI; rinse pressure approx 20 PSI regulated via booster pump or pressure reducing valve; pump curve behavior increasing pressure when flow restricted due to wash arm blockage.

Tank Capacity: LX wash tank volume approx 2.9 gal.

Drain System: drain hose approx 5/8"–7/8" ID pumped drain; gravity drain approx 2" pipe; typical drain flow approx 4 GPM; minimum drain capacity approx 5 GPM; LX pumped drain discharge loop height ≤38" above finished floor.

Water Connections: inlet connection 1/2" FPT undercounter / door machines; 3/4" FPT conveyor machines.

Heater Systems: immersion heaters Incoloy sheathed; LX tank heater approx 1.5–1.8 kW maintaining wash tank temperature approx 150–160°F; LX booster heater approx 4.9–6 kW producing final rinse approx 180°F; AM-15 / AM-16 tank heater approx 5.0 kW; AM booster heater approx 8.5–13 kW; CL44e tank heater approx 15 kW; CL44e booster heater approx 15–30 kW configurable.

Electrical Heater Control: booster heater on-demand activation during rinse phase; temperature interlock logic via Sense-A-Temp; heater contactor control via controller logic.

Sensors and Control Feedback: thermistor temperature sensors approx 10 kΩ at 77°F reference; sump pressure switch for tank level detection; door magnetic safety switch interlock; controller monitors temperature, water level, and cycle status.

Cycle Performance: LX series productivity approx 32 racks/hr; typical cycle duration approx 109 s; LXe cycle programs approx Light 120 s, Normal 146 s, Heavy 275 s; throughput approx 30 / 24 / 13 racks/hr depending cycle.

Chemical Systems: integrated dosing pumps detergent pump rinse aid pump sanitizer pump (chemical models) delime pump; sanitizer sodium hypochlorite approx 6% solution achieving approx 50–100 ppm concentration.

Water Quality Requirements: recommended hardness ≤3 grains per gallon; water treatment systems softener RO system iron filter sediment filtration.

Electrical Configuration: example LXiC 120 V approx 15.4 A; LXiH 208–240 V approx 37.7 A.

Control System Architecture: microcomputer control board managing wash pump rinse pump heater control chemical dosing cycle timing temperature verification rinse temperature interlock service diagnostics.
